## 📖 Synopsis & Visual Style
**Kaito Adventure: Ink Shinobi** immerses players directly into the living pages of a classic Japanese shonen action manga. Every screen, combat effect, interface overlay, and boss encounter is crafted with traditional comic book art and bold 2D cel-shaded vector techniques:
- **Halftone & Screentones**: Authentic print textures across backgrounds, clouds, and panel borders.
- **Speed & Action Lines**: Dynamic radial and horizontal concentration lines during dashes, boss charges, and critical slashes.
- **Dynamic Manga Sound Effects**: Stylized onomatopoeias popping right out of the battlefield (*SLASH!*, *DODODO!*, *SHINK!*, *BAM!*, *BOING!*, *ゴゴゴゴ*, *ズバッ!*).
- **Comic Prologue & Epilogue Panels**: Interactive story introduction at the start of the journey and a canonical narrative conclusion unlocked upon vanquishing the True Final Boss in Chapter 10.
- **Speech Balloons & Dramatic Anime Cut-Ins**: Manga cutscenes triggered during special ability executions and boss phase transitions.
- **Responsive Panel-Style HUD**: Inked heart containers, Secret Technique meter (*Ougi / 奥義*), hit combo counter, and adaptive PvP duel/Battle Royale health bars centered across phones, tablets, and widescreen displays.

## ⚔️ Local WiFi PvP Multiplayer (Up to 5 Players)
The game features a complete real-time local network multiplayer system with zero account creation or cloud server requirements:
- **Instant Room Discovery & Direct IP Connection**: Automatic UDP beacon room discovery across the same WiFi network, alongside direct IP join support (fully cross-compatible between Windows, Android, and Web).
- **3 Selectable PvP Arenas**:
  - **Stage 1 — Pagoda Dojo**: Classic single-platform Final Destination-style duel stage.
  - **Stage 2 — Crimson Citadel**: Multi-platform battlefield with elevated side perches and a central top platform over blazing fortress skies.
  - **Stage 3 — Frostfang Sanctuary**: Dynamic aerial layout featuring side platforms and a moving top platform across frozen peaks.
- **Host Match Configuration**:
  - **Match Timer**: Choose between Unlimited (`∞`), `1:30`, `3:00`, or `5:00` timed battles with automatic sudden-death/tiebreaker resolution.
  - **Health Pool**: Configure player vitality between `150 HP`, `300 HP`, or `500 HP`.
  - **Stock Lives**: Set Campaign-style stock lives (`1`, `2`, `3`, or `5` lives per shinobi) with automatic respawn invulnerability.
- **Shinobi Customization**: Choose your custom nickname and personalize Kaito's outfit with an 8-color multiplayer skin system that recolors Kaito's headband, scarf, kimono, and katana aura while preserving natural skin tones and ground shadows.
- **Balanced PvP Combat & Smash-Style Ring-Out**:
  - Real-time network synchronization for 3-hit sword combos, kunai projectiles, and the *Celestial Dragon-Ink Flurry* Ultimate (*Ougi*).
  - **Smash Bros-Style Ring-Out Blast**: Knocking an opponent off the stage triggers a directional comic energy beam explosion, camera shake, and *RING OUT!!* callout.
  - Full stat, health, and stock-life reset at the start of every new match and rematch.
- **Arena Celebration & Winner Podium**:
  - 3.2-second in-arena victory celebration sequence for the champion before entering the podium.
  - Interactive 10-second rematch countdown when two or more players vote ready.

## 🎮 Controls

The game offers full support for both **PC Keyboard** and **On-Screen Virtual Touch Controls**:

| Action | PC Key | On-Screen Button | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Left / Right** | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` | `[ ◄ ]` / `[ ► ]` | Agile and responsive horizontal movement |
| **Jump / Wall Jump** | `Space` / `W` / `↑` | `[ JUMP ]` | Variable height jump and wall rebound |
| **Sword Combo** | `J` or `X` | `[ SLASH ]` | 3-hit melee combo with vibrant ink slash arcs |
| **Air Dash / Dodge** | `K` / `C` / `Shift` | `[ DASH ]` | Supersonic dodge with invulnerability frames (i-frames) |
| **Throw Kunai** | `L` or `Z` | `[ KUNAI ]` | Ranged shuriken attack with a 5-second tactical cooldown |
| **Secret Technique (奥義 Ougi)** | `U` or `V` | `[ OUGI ]` | *Celestial Dragon-Ink Flurry* — 8-frame omnislash & screen-cleaving vortex (100% Spirit) |
| **Pause / Menu** | `ESC` or `P` | `[ ⏸ ]` | Pauses the action to access settings or restart |

## 🏯 Story Mode: 10 Chapters, 6 Enemy Types & 10 Bosses

### 🌙 Chapter 1: The Inked Rooftops (*墨の屋根*)
- **Setting**: Traditional pagoda rooftops beneath a giant crescent moon, spike traps, and crumbling platforms.
- **Boss: KUROKAGE (The Shadow Executioner)**: Smoke-bomb teleportation, shadow blade waves, and a counter posture that requires heavy combo strikes to shatter (*GUARD BREAK*).

### 🌋 Chapter 2: The Crimson Citadel (*紅蓮の城塞*)
- **Setting**: A blazing warlord fortress filled with spinning buzzsaws, spike pits, and magma vents.
- **Boss: RYU-KOTEI (The Dragon Shogun)**: Dual-shockwave seismic stomps, telegraphed flame geysers erupting from the floor, and a berserk crimson rage mode.

### 🐍 Chapter 3: The Serpentine Sanctum (*蛇神の聖域*)
- **Setting**: An underground temple covered in toxic mist and swinging platforms suspended over bottomless chasms.
- **Boss: HEBI-HIME (The Serpent Empress)**: Serpentine Kusarigama lunges, homing venom spheres, and optical illusions.

### ❄️ Chapter 4: The Frostfang Peak (*霜牙の頂*)
- **Setting**: Blizzard-swept mountain peaks with horizontal crosswinds and slippery ice platforms.
- **Boss: YUKI-ONI (The Frost Demon)**: Colossal Kanabo glacier slams, falling icicles, and visibility-reducing blizzards.

### 🌑 Chapter 5: The Shadow Palace (*影の宮殿*)
- **Setting**: The dark citadel featuring disappearing platforms and rhythmic switch orbs.
- **Boss: MUZAN-KAI (Lord of the Blood Eclipse)**: Dual cursed Odachi slashes, dimensional rifts, and blood-eclipse barriers.

### ⚙️ Chapter 6: The Clockwork Foundry (*絡繰の鍛冶場*)
- **Setting**: A towering steampunk Karakuri fortress with brass conveyor platforms, rotating sawblades, and steam vents.
- **New Enemies**: Clockwork Karakuri Automatons and Armored Samurai Shield-Bearers.
- **Boss: GENJI-KARAKURI (The Clockwork Shogun)**: Twin steam-saw halberd cleaves, overdrive piston charges, and spinning brass cogwheel barrages.

### 🦊 Chapter 7: The Kitsune Mirage Shrine (*九尾の幻영社*)
- **Setting**: A mystical autumn torii sanctuary bathed in violet spirit lanterns, floating illusion platforms, and bouncy Taiko drums.
- **New Enemies**: Kitsune Spirit Mages casting homing foxfire orbs.
- **Boss: INARI-KYUBI (The Nine-Tail Archmage)**: Phantom spirit dashes, radial nine-tail foxfire rings, and vertical spirit pillars.

### 🌊 Chapter 8: The Abyssal Dragon Palace (*深淵の竜宮城*)
- **Setting**: A sunken bioluminescent Ryugu-jo palace with abyssal currents, coral spires, and treacherous depths.
- **Boss: WADATSUMI (The Abyssal Leviathan)**: Tidal trident charges, anchor slams that erupt abyssal water geysers, and twin whirlpool shockwaves.

### ⚡ Chapter 9: The Thundercloud Bastion (*雷帝の天城*)
- **Setting**: A celestial storm citadel suspended among lightning clouds, high-altitude wind currents, and electrified platforms.
- **Boss: RAIJIN-TEI (The Thunder Emperor)**: Supersonic lightning katanas, Taiko thunder-drum sky bolts, and storm-shield counters.

### 🌌 Chapter 10: The Astral Void Throne (*虚空の神座*)
- **Setting**: The primordial dimension at the edge of creation where ink, stars, and gravity collide.
- **True Final Boss: AKUMA-SHIN (The Primordial Ink God)**: Eight-winged cosmic deity wielding dual Supernova Odachi blades, astral meteor storms, void rifts, and a 3-phase final showdown that unlocks the canonical **Story Epilogue**.

## 🚀 How to Run & Play

### Option 1: Play in Web Browser (Online PWA)
Play directly in your browser with automatic landscape orientation support on mobile devices and desktop:
👉 **[kaito-adventure-beta.vercel.app](https://kaito-adventure-beta.vercel.app/)**

Or run locally in development:
```bash
flutter run -d chrome
```

### Option 2: Windows Native Desktop (PC)
Pre-built portable 64-bit release packaged in the `releases/` directory:
* Extract: `releases/Kaito-Adventure-Windows.zip`
* Run: `manga_platformer.exe`

To build from source:
```bash
flutter build windows --release
```

### Option 3: Install on Android (Optimized APKs)
Pre-compiled APK packages available in the `releases/` directory:
* **Recommended (ARM64-v8a)**: `releases/Kaito-Adventure-Android-arm64.apk` (~39 MB, optimized for modern 64-bit Android smartphones).
* **Universal**: `releases/Kaito-Adventure-Android-Universal.apk` (~72 MB, compatible with all Android architectures).

To build from source:
```bash
flutter build apk --release --split-per-abi
```
